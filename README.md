# Tire Tread Wear & Replacement Safety Checker

Enter tread depth at inner, center, and outer groove for each of four tires; the app flags readings against legal (1.6 mm) and recommended (3 mm) safety thresholds with colour-coded indicators. Per-tire analysis diagnoses uneven wear patterns (centre, edge, camber) with likely causes, estimates remaining kilometres to the legal limit, and assigns an overall replacement urgency rating.
